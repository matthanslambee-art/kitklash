KITKLASH runs on a made-to-order model. You order any time; if it's not already on hand, your order joins our next monthly supplier run and reaches you in around two weeks. No endless "coming soon" restocks — just a straight line from order to terrace.

01

You Order

Place your order any time. If it's on hand, it ships straight away. If it's not, your order queues for our next supplier run — no guesswork, no waiting on a restock that may never come.

02

We Order

On the 25th of every month, every order placed that month is bundled together and sent to our supplier as one batch. Order any time before the 25th to make that month's run.

03

You Receive

Once the monthly order is placed with our supplier, delivery takes around two weeks to reach you. We'll keep you posted the whole way.

Shipping & Returns

Complimentary express worldwide shipping on all archival pieces. Due to the rare nature of these items, returns are only accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the security tag remains attached and intact.
