Privacy & Terms

Plain language, not a wall of legal text. If anything here is unclear, WhatsApp or email us and we'll explain it properly.

What we collect

When you order, request a jersey, or sign up for our newsletter, we collect what's needed to act on that: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, and delivery address for orders. That's it — we don't collect anything beyond what a specific action requires.

Payments

Card payments are handled entirely by Yoco, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number — Yoco's own secure checkout page takes your payment details directly, and we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.

Who sees your information

Only us and Yoco (for processing payment). We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with any other third party. Your cart is stored locally in your own browser, not on our servers, until you actually place an order.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (including unsubscribing from the newsletter at any time) — just message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll sort it out directly.

Orders, shipping & returns

Most jerseys are made to order — your order joins the next monthly batch (placed on the 25th) and arrives roughly two weeks after that. Anything marked "Ready to Ship" goes out immediately instead. Delivery is within South Africa. Got a problem with your order? Reach out and we'll sort it out — every case is handled individually.

Replicas, not originals

KITKLASH sells true-to-original replica football jerseys. We do not claim any item is an authenticated, club-issued original.
